Still

Borrow the color. Keep the moment.

A small color-grading studio that brings the palette of a reference image to your own photograph, without changing what is in the frame.

Try Still No account. Your images stay on your device.
Platform
Web browser
Built with
TypeScript, AssemblyScript, WebAssembly
Status
Working prototype

Start with a feeling

A photograph can have the right composition but a different mood from the one you have in mind. Still starts with a reference: an image whose colors you like. It uses that palette to give your photograph a different atmosphere while keeping its content intact.

A short path from reference to result

  1. Choose a reference image and the photograph you want to grade.
  2. Adjust the transfer strength, or keep the original brightness.
  3. Move the comparison slider to see the difference, then export a PNG at your photograph’s original dimensions.

Built to stay in the browser

Still uses Reinhard color transfer in the Lab color space, compiled to WebAssembly. A Web Worker runs the processing away from the interface, while smaller previews keep adjustments responsive. There is no AI model or image-processing backend, and no upload step.

Where it works, and where it stops

This is global color matching. It does not recognise faces, skies, or individual objects, so a strong transfer can shift skin tones or clip vivid colors. Lowering the strength can help. The prototype accepts PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 8 megapixels and 25 MB; it is not a RAW or HDR editor.
